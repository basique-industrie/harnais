Privacy

Harnais is maintained by Jean Humann. This policy describes the macOS application and its shared connections.

Data on your Mac

Harnais creates local coding-account profiles and runs provider command-line tools. It reads local credentials where needed to identify accounts and retrieve usage or quota information directly from those providers.

OAuth access and refresh tokens, custom OAuth app credentials, and Grafana service-account tokens are stored under ~/.harnais with restricted file permissions. They are not sent to a Harnais server. Each connection has its own credentials.

Connected services and coding tools

When you connect a service, your browser opens the service’s authorization page. The service returns authorization to Harnais on your Mac. Harnais uses OAuth PKCE where supported. It shares public app identifiers across installations, never another user’s access tokens.

When an enabled coding tool requests service data through the local MCP adapter, Harnais sends the request directly to that service and returns the response to the requesting tool. That tool and its AI provider may process the returned data under their own policies. Enable connections only for tools you intend to grant access to.

Google Drive permissions support reading and searching Drive files, plus creating, modifying, and deleting the specific files granted to this app through the drive.file scope. Gmail permissions support reading and searching email. Outlook uses delegated Microsoft Graph Mail.Read to list, search, and read the signed-in user’s messages; it does not send, modify, or delete mail. Slack permissions depend on the app registration and consent screen. The default registration supports reading and searching content available to the authorizing user. Custom workspace registrations can also allow sending or scheduling messages, reactions, file uploads, and canvas or list changes to preserve existing tool capabilities. Atlassian permissions follow the service’s consent screen. Grafana access follows the service account’s permissions.

Use of Google data

Harnais uses Google data only to provide the connected features you request. Harnais does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Harnais’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Telemetry and website

The app includes no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking, crash-upload service, or Harnais telemetry endpoint. This static website loads no analytics scripts. GitHub hosts the site and processes requests under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Disconnecting and deletion

Disconnect a service in Harnais to remove its local credentials and configured Harnais adapter entries. Revoke Harnais in the service’s account settings to invalidate access at the service. To remove all Harnais local data, quit the app and remove ~/.harnais. Vendor account directories remain separate.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a support issue without including private data or credentials.
