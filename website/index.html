Harnais is a macOS app for managing Claude, Codex, Cursor, and OpenCode accounts. Shared service connections let your local coding tools use the same service login.

One Harnais app. Your own access.

You authorize your own account with the service. Harnais keeps that account’s credentials on your Mac and provides a local MCP adapter for your coding tools. Other Harnais users cannot access your connections.

Connections

Google Drive, Gmail, and Slack connections are being prepared for public availability. Google Drive uses a local adapter for the stable Drive API; Developer Preview eligibility is not required. Gmail uses a local read-only adapter for the Gmail API. Outlook uses Microsoft Graph for read-only mail search and reading, with personal and work Microsoft accounts. Slack public access requires Marketplace approval. Atlassian uses its remote MCP service. Grafana uses a service-account token you provide.

Tools bundled with a coding provider are labeled Built-in. Tools installed in a provider are Added. Logins managed by Harnais for reuse on your Mac are Shared.

Availability and support

Harnais is in development. OAuth app registration does not mean an integration has been approved for public use. For setup questions or issues, contact the maintainer through GitHub. Do not post tokens, passwords, or private service data.
